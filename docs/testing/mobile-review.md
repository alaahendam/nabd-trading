## What changes at merge (from the plan, section 5)

- **Remove the `git.deploymentEnabled` entry for `feat/mobile` from `apps/web/vercel.json`.** It exists only to stop Vercel deploying this branch, and the plan says it is removed again at the merge. It is still there.
- **The pull request** (#1) is open, not a draft, and GitHub reports its state as `unstable` at the time of writing; that is worth reading before merging.
- **The tag `v1.1.0`** is the author's call, and only on the plan's condition.

## Verdict

The code, the tests and CI are where the plan wanted them. The plan's own bar has two items not met (the small and large emulator screens, and the Maestro flows), three met only partly (Dynamic Type on an emulator, the icon and splash on the APK, the language switch on the APK), and two that are the author's to deliver (the statement and the screenshots). By the plan's wording, the bar for merging and tagging `v1.1.0` is therefore **not yet fully met**. Whether to merge with those gaps documented, close them first, or leave the branch as a documented experimental branch is the author's decision.
